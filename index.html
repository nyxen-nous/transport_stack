About the data

Scheduled journey times

This pilot covers Delhi routes 85 and 990 in both directions. Times come from an archived timetable and include the wait after your chosen departure time. Actual bus arrivals can vary.

How to read ticket demand

Each label compares a route segment with its usual weekday or weekend demand. Estimates use ONDC e-tickets from October 1–24, 2026, grouped into half-hour periods. Seat availability is unknown.

Where coverage is limited

Only 6.65% of training tickets on these routes matched a single route pattern. A journey shows “Limited data” when any segment lacks enough observations. In the held-out week, 45% of supported weekday estimates kept the same label.
